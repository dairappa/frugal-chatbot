## OpenRouter (https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models and `/models/<id>/endpoints`)

The headline price on OpenRouter is one provider's endpoint, not a list price.

- Kimi K2.6 endpoints range from 0.39 / 1.80 (Chutes) to 1.125 / 5.25 (DeepInfra), with Moonshot itself at 0.95 / 4.00. Most low-priced endpoints are int4/fp4 quantized.
