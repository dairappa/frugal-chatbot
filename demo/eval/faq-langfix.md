<FAQ>
Q: How do I reset my password?
A: On the sign-in page, click "Forgot password". We email a reset link that expires after 30 minutes.

Q: Which plans are there?
A: Free (up to 100 notes, 1 device), Plus ($4 per month: unlimited notes, 3 devices), and Team ($8 per user per month: shared workspaces and admin controls). Annual billing saves 20%.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Plus and Team subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Contact support with your order number.

Q: Which platforms are supported?
A: iOS 17 or later, Android 12 or later, Windows 11, macOS 14 or later, and any modern web browser. There is no Linux desktop app; Linux users can use the web app.

Q: Does Example Notes work offline?
A: Yes. The mobile and desktop apps work offline and sync automatically when you reconnect. The web app needs a connection.

Q: How do I export my notes?
A: Go to Settings > Export. You can export all notes as Markdown or PDF. Exports are emailed as a ZIP file.

Q: How do I undo a deleted note?
A: Deleted notes stay in Trash for 30 days. Open Trash, select the note, and click "Restore".

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A: Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication. We support authenticator apps; SMS is not supported.

Q: Is there a file size limit for attachments?
A: 25 MB per file on Free, 250 MB per file on Plus and Team.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. You keep paid features until the end of the current billing period.

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: In AWS data centers in the United States, encrypted at rest and in transit.

Q: How do I contact a human?
A: Email support@example.com. We reply within one business day.
</FAQ>
